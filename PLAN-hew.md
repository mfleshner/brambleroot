# Plan: cooking, and Hew the Almighty Wizard

Matt's idea (Nov 9, 2026). Three parts, one session each. Part 1 stands on its own.

## Part 1: cooking — DONE Nov 9, 2026
Shipped as planned: 17 dishes (3 starters, 10 recipe cards, 4 letters), 2 a day, hearty ones 15 past max. econ4: cooking comes out 2 to 8% under not cooking, since beds, not energy, cap earnings past early spring. It helps relaxed days and Hollow dives.


Today energy only comes back when she sleeps (plus Isolde's breakfast as a spouse). Cooking adds a way to refill it during the day.

- **The kitchen** is a second tab on the Brew sheet: Cauldron | Kitchen. Cooking is instant and takes 10 minutes of game time. Dishes go to her pouch.
- **About 16 dishes** from crops, fish, foraging, honey and a few Hollow drops. Each refills 15–60 energy, never above her max. A few are "hearty": they raise today's energy cap by 20 as well.
- **Fullness:** she can eat 2 dishes a day (3 once she has a bigger appetite from a later reward). This is what keeps cooking from turning into endless energy.
- **Eat anywhere**, including in the Hollow, from the pouch. That makes dishes useful for deep dives too.
- **Recipes:** 3 starters come with the kitchen (a letter from Isolde on the first morning). The rest are recipe cards in the Market, plus a few from villager letters at 3 hearts.
- **Sell value** is always below the ingredients, so cooking is never a money maker. Villagers can still be given dishes as gifts, and some like them.
- **Balance check:** run econ4 with a bot that cooks and eats its best dish twice a day. Season earnings may rise a little (more work done), but not enough to make charters trivial. Report the numbers.

## Part 2: Hew the Almighty Wizard (the passive villain) — DONE Nov 9, 2026
Shipped with Part 3 in one go. The tower is at the right edge of the Old Wood. A curse that starts mid-season (when he first arrives) weakens at least six days later. Counters per season: spring Calming Draught/Hearth Tonic/Dreamwater, summer Sunfire Salve/Thornberry Jam/Melon Cordial, autumn Wolf's Ward/Amber Syrup/Chanterelle Tonic, winter Frost Elixir/Minnow Fry. The ease setting is a button on the Sleep sheet.


Hew is Matt. He looks like Matt: brown hair swept up and back, a full brown beard with a touch of grey, heavy dark brows, blue-grey eyes, a smug grin. Tall pointed wizard hat, deep robe, a dusty-rose sash. He gets a portrait like the villagers (moods: smug, annoyed, flustered, fond) and a tower at the edge of the map she can walk to.

- **One curse a season.** On day 1 a gloating letter from Hew names it. One at a time, always mild, never enough to sink a charter.
- **It can't be broken early.** On a random day from 7 to 14 the curse weakens (a note from a villager says so). Only from then on does the counter work. Before that, the counter item does nothing ("Hew's spell holds firm. Not yet.").
- **The counter** is a specific potion, dish or offering, hinted at in town gossip once it weakens. She takes it to Hew's tower. The curse lifts, Hew grumbles, and she gets a small reward. Unbroken curses end with the season anyway.
- **About 8 curses**, rotating so she doesn't see a repeat for two years. Ideas:
  - Crops take one extra day to grow.
  - The fish are shy (harder to catch).
  - No rain all season (she waters everything).
  - Mornings start an hour later.
  - The watering can holds less.
  - The market pays a little less for raw herbs (potions unaffected).
  - Forage spots are fewer.
  - The cauldron has one fewer pot.
- **Gossip:** villagers comment on the current curse and on Hew. He's smug and dramatic and a bit of a joke in town.
- **An "ease up" setting** in Settings turns the curse effects off (letters and story still happen), in case one is annoying her.

## Part 3: winning him over, and romance — DONE Nov 9, 2026
The duel: he names his spell in his taunt (Fireball, Tidal Wave, Rockfall, Whirlwind) and she taps the element that beats it; first to three, one try a day. He secretly loves Starfruit Tart, Truffle Risotto and Hearty Gourd Soup.


- **Warming up.** Every curse she breaks raises Hew's hearts. He goes from nemesis to rival to grudging friend. Visiting the tower and giving him dishes he secretly loves help a little; ordinary gifts barely move him.
- **Harder than the other romances:**
  - Hearts come mainly from breaking curses, so it takes a long time.
  - He won't date her until she has broken at least four curses (about a full in-game year).
  - A proposal needs a final wizard's duel she has to win.
- **If she is already married to someone else,** Hew stays a friend (Matt's call). He never becomes a rival to her spouse.
- **If they do marry,** the real curses stop. He still sends a harmless joke curse now and then.
- Story scenes at his heart levels, like the other villagers.
